Method and boundaries

This synthesis joins Stress test frontier analysis, Estimate global AI investment since, and Analyze Pace the frontier. It also incorporates the expanded project collection and the sibling Economic Impact AGI notes. The conversations establish the questions and research history; they are not independent corroboration of factual claims.

Key event, financial, and policy claims were checked against primary records for this edition. Several contextual references are carried from the earlier project audits or supplied transcripts and are identified below. The Ulanqab source could not be fully re-extracted; its currency correction is explicitly retained as a prior-audit finding. Exact model rankings, social-media quotations, and funding-network allegations that were not independently established are not promoted to fact.

Company disclosures establish what an issuer reports. Government statements and legal records have defined institutional scopes. Expert advocacy, empirical studies, forecasts, and philosophical essays answer different questions. Evidence labels indicate the type of support, not a mechanical score of credibility.

The new article is approximately 5,667 words before figures and reference material. It condenses the collection’s arguments; it does not claim to reproduce every statement. One exact duplicate and companion formats are consolidated. Private task logs and full third-party clippings are not distributed in this public repository.
